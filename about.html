About Palmreach

Support that's personal. Local. Reliable.

We believe good support starts with listening, respecting individual choice and building relationships that participants can rely on.

Our approach

People first, every time.

Palmreach Support Services provides person-centred disability support across Cairns and surrounding communities. We focus on dignity, choice, control, inclusion and practical support that fits naturally into each participant's life.

  • Listen to the participant's goals and preferences
  • Promote independence rather than dependence
  • Communicate clearly with participants and their support networks
  • Build consistent, trusted support relationships
  • Respect cultural, language and individual differences

Experienced local leadership

Japneet Singh brings approximately seven years of disability-support experience and leads Palmreach with a focus on reliability, responsiveness and participant-centred care.

The broader team includes experience and training relevant to disability support, First Aid, CPR, manual handling and infection control.

Our values

What guides our support

Respect

Every person is treated with dignity, privacy and respect.

Choice

Participants remain at the centre of decisions about their support.

Reliability

We value consistency, clear communication and doing what we say we'll do.

Independence

Support should build capability, confidence and control wherever possible.

Inclusion

We support meaningful participation and connection in community life.

Community

Local knowledge helps us connect people with opportunities around them.
