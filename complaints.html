Complaints & Feedback

We welcome feedback.

Feedback, compliments and complaints help Palmreach improve. Raising a complaint will not negatively affect a person's right to receive fair and respectful services.

How to contact us

You can contact Palmreach by phone on 0476 473 703 or by email at palmreach@outlook.com.au.

What happens next

Palmreach aims to acknowledge named feedback and complaints promptly, keep the person informed where appropriate and work toward a fair resolution.

Independent options

Participants and representatives may also seek information about external complaint pathways, including the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission, where appropriate.
