Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This public website is designed to minimise the amount of personal information collected online.

Website enquiries

The website's initial enquiry and referral forms are intentionally limited. Please do not submit NDIS numbers, diagnoses, medical records or other sensitive health information through the public website.

How enquiries work

In the low-cost static version of this website, enquiry forms prepare an email on the visitor's own device. The website itself does not maintain a participant database or store the information entered into those forms.

Further information

Once Palmreach has made contact, any further information required to assess or deliver supports should be handled through appropriate secure processes and in line with Palmreach's privacy and record-management obligations.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be directed to Palmreach Support Services at palmreach@outlook.com.au or 0476 473 703.
