Our Services

Flexible support built around everyday life.

We provide a broad range of registered NDIS supports, with service delivery subject to individual needs, compatibility, risk assessment, worker competency, staffing and available funding.

Participant-friendly support

Services designed around your goals

⌂

Personal Activities

Assistance with daily routines and personal activities delivered respectfully and according to individual preferences.

♿

Travel & Transport

Support to travel to appointments, activities and community destinations, including wheelchair-accessible transport where suitable.

☀

Community Participation

Support to take part in social, recreational and community activities and build meaningful connections.

↗

Development of Life Skills

Practical support to build confidence with daily routines, shopping, cooking, travel and community participation.

✓

Household Tasks

Help with suitable household activities that support a safe, functional home environment.

◉

Daily Tasks / Shared Living

Support with day-to-day living needs consistent with the participant's plan and support arrangements.

✦

High-Intensity Personal Activities

Available only where appropriate assessments, competencies and safeguards are in place and within Palmreach's registration scope.

▣

Community Nursing Care

Where clinically appropriate and within scope, nursing-related supports may be considered following assessment.

★

Employment Support

Support for participants seeking to access, maintain or develop skills relevant to employment and workplace participation.
